Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Short version: we collect your email and your orders, and nothing else. No ads, no tracking pixels, no selling data.

What we collect

Your email, when you join our list or make an account, so we can send Butter Spread, plugin news and your licences. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Your orders and licences, when you buy or start a trial: name, email, what you bought, the computers a licence is activated on, and the country needed for tax.

Who handles it

Our store and licensing partner runs checkout, accounts and licences, and processes payments with its payment provider. Card details go to the payment provider, never to us.

Google keeps our email list (Google Sheets) and sends our list emails from our Google account. Zoho Mail handles our chef@ mailbox.

GoatCounter counts page visits so we know which pages people read. It uses no cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and stores no personal data.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs (like IP address) for security.

Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, so your browser asks Google for them.

On your device

The site remembers your basket and whether sound is on, in your own browser's storage. It is not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to see, correct or delete your data. Write to chef@pluginsforbreakfast.com and we will reply within 30 days. We keep order records only as long as tax law requires.
