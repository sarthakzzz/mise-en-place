Mac Apple-notarised

Every Mac installer is signed with our Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so macOS opens it straight away. No trips to System Settings.

  1. Open the installer you downloaded from your account.
  2. Click Continue until it says it's done. AU, VST3 and AAX (Pro Tools) all go in together.
  3. That's breakfast served.

Seeing a warning anyway? You probably have an older copy from before 12 November 2026. Download it again from your account and the fresh one opens cleanly. Still stuck? Write to us.

Windows About 1 minute

The message is a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC". Windows shows it for any new app it hasn't met many times yet. It isn't a virus warning.

  1. Got a .zip? Right-click it, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom if it's there, click OK, then right-click and choose Extract All.
  2. Run the setup file. If the blue box appears, click More info.
  3. Check the app name is one of our plugins, then click Run anyway.
  4. Click through the installer. Your plugin lands in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

If your antivirus pops it into quarantine, don't switch your protection off. Write to us and we'll sort it out together.

After installing

  1. Open (or restart) your DAW. Our plugins are under Plugins for Breakfast.
  2. Not there? Rescan. Logic: Settings > Plug-in Manager > Reset & Rescan. Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins, turn on VST3 system folders, Rescan. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins.
  3. Making music in the browser? veena loads your installed VST3 and AU plugins through its free veena bridge app. Install the bridge, and our plugins show up in your veena studio.
  4. Open the plugin, click Activate and sign in with the email you bought with. Bon appétit!
