Installing safely

Your computer is a careful eater. The first time you install one of our plugins it may sniff the plate and show a warning. Here's why, and how to dig in, in about a minute.

The honest bit: we're a tiny kitchen, and our Apple and Windows signing is still in the oven. Until it's done, your Mac or PC simply doesn't recognise our name yet, so it plays it safe. The files are exactly what we cooked, nothing added.

Mac About 1 minute

The message usually says the installer "can't be opened", or that "Apple could not verify" it is free of malware.

  1. Open the installer you downloaded. When the warning appears, click Done. Don't move it to the Bin.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security part.
  3. Next to the message about our installer, click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password, then click Open.
  4. Click Continue until the installer says it's done. That's breakfast served.

On macOS before Sequoia (14 or older) there's a shortcut: right-click (or Control-click) the installer, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows About 1 minute

The message is a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC". Windows shows it for any new app it hasn't met many times yet. It isn't a virus warning.

  1. Got a .zip? Right-click it, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom if it's there, click OK, then right-click and choose Extract All.
  2. Run the setup file. If the blue box appears, click More info.
  3. Check the app name is one of our plugins, then click Run anyway.
  4. Click through the installer. Your plugin lands in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

If your antivirus pops it into quarantine, don't switch your protection off. Write to us and we'll sort it out together.

After installing

  1. Open (or restart) your DAW. Our plugins are under Plugins for Breakfast.
  2. Not there? Rescan. Logic: Settings > Plug-in Manager > Reset & Rescan. Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins, turn on VST3 system folders, Rescan. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins.
  3. Open the plugin, click Activate and sign in with the email you bought with. Bon appétit!
