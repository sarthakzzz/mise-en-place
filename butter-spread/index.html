Butter Spread

Our stereo widener. Spread a mix wide and smooth, keep the bass solid in the middle, and it still sounds right in mono.

  • Mac, Apple Silicon and Intel
  • AU and VST3
  • Open source (AGPLv3)

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded .pkg file and follow the steps. If macOS says it can't check the file, right-click it and choose Open.
  2. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. Butter Spread shows up as an AU and a VST3 effect.
