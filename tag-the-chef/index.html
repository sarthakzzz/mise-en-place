Tag the Chef

Cook something with our plugins, post it, tag us, and pick one plugin on us. The free 3-day trial counts.

How it works

  1. Make a video using any Plugins for Breakfast plugin. A trial counts. We need to be able to hear or see the plugin.
  2. Post it publicly on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube (Shorts too), X or Threads. Tag @pluginsforbreakfast (on X: @PFBAudio) and add #TagTheChef.
  3. Email chef@pluginsforbreakfast.com with the link to your post, the email of your Plugins for Breakfast account, and the plugin you want.
  4. We check it within 2 days and send you a gift code. Open it on pluginsforbreakfast.com, sign in, and the plugin is yours to keep.

  • One free plugin per person.

  • Runs from 15 November 2026 to 31 January 2027. We can end or change it at any time.
