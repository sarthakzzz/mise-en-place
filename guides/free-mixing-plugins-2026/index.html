Free mixing plugins in 2026: try all 16 of ours free

Free should mean the full meal, not a cut-down teaser. Every plugin on our menu can be tasted in full for 3 days, and one of them stays on the house.

By The Chef, Plugins for Breakfast · 13 November 2026

1All 16 plugins, free for 3 days

Every plugin on our menu has a full 3-day trial with nothing locked: make a free account, download, and use everything. No card needed. When you buy, the same install unlocks and your presets stay put.

There is distortion, glitch, EQ, compression, mastering, delay, reverb, a performance multi-effect, a clipper, an auto-pan, a gate, chorus, tape, a filter and a granular effect. Each one is a little needle-felt world you play like a toy.

2Butter Spread: a free stereo widener

Using Butter Spread, our free stereo widener plugin

Butter Spread widens your mix from mono up to 200% with the knife, adds a mono-safe doubler with the cinnamon shaker, and keeps bass in mono below 40 to 400 Hz with the toaster lever. It is open source (AGPLv3) and comes as AU and VST3 on Mac and VST3 on Windows.

We're sending a few free licences to our customers. Would you like to try it? Sign up on the Butter Spread page and one is yours.

3Use it on: synths, pads and backing vocals

Wide, airy parts love width. Keep your kick, bass and lead vocal centred, and spread the things around them. Then check in mono: thanks to the toaster lever and the mono-safe doubler, the low end and the doubled parts stay solid.
