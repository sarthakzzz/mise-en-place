A vocal chain for beginners

Vocals are the main course. A few plugins in the right order make them sit on top of the beat, clear and confident, instead of floating on it or hiding under it.

By The Chef, Plugins for Breakfast · 13 November 2026

1Shape the tone

Using Spice Shelf EQ, our EQ plugin

Start by cleaning up: a little less boom, a little more presence.

In Spice Shelf EQ, lower Hearty if the vocal is boomy, lift Zesty for presence, and use Tame to smooth harsh S sounds and peaks. Watch the live curve to see what you changed.

2Keep it steady

Using Popcorn Sandwich Compressor, our compressor plugin

Compression evens out loud and quiet words so the vocal stays in front.

Turn the Popcorn Sandwich dial left until the loudest words are tamed. Smooth Opto is gentle and musical on voices; Punch FET is more aggressive for rap.

3Echo without the mud

Using Spaghetti Delay, our delay plugin

A ducked delay adds depth but stays out of the way of the words.

Use Spaghetti Delay with a synced 1/4 or dotted 1/8 and turn up the cloche (ducking).

4Space that stays clear

Using Bread Oven Reverb, our reverb plugin

Reverb on a vocal can wash it out. Pre-delay and ducking keep it clear.

In Bread Oven Reverb use the focaccia (plate) for a classic vocal sound, add some hourglass (pre-delay) so the words land before the reverb, and turn on the duck so the tail dips while the singer sings.

5Widen the backing vocals

Using Milkshake Chorus, our chorus plugin

Keep the lead vocal centred and widen the doubles and harmonies around it.

Milkshake Chorus with two straws and a little width thickens backing vocals. For a simple, mono-safe double, Butter Spread's cinnamon shaker is free.

Questions from the dining room

What order should vocal plugins go in?

A common order is EQ, then compression, then time effects (delay and reverb) on sends. Some producers EQ again after compression.

Should reverb go on an insert or a send?

A send is usually better: you can share one reverb across tracks and filter or duck it without touching the dry vocal.

Why does my vocal sound thin after EQ?

Too much low cut or too much boost up top. Bring some Hearty back, and compare at the same loudness.
