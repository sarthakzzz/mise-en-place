Getting it in the oven

How do I install a plugin?

1. Download it from your account (My plugins). Pick Mac or Windows.

2. Run the installer. Mac: open the installer and click through. Windows: open the zip and run the setup.

3. If your computer warns you (only the first time). Mac: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Windows: click More info, then Run anyway.

4. Open your DAW (restart it if it was open). The plugin is under Plugins for Breakfast.

5. Unlock it. Open the plugin, click Activate and sign in with the email you bought with. That's it.

My DAW doesn't show the plugin

Restart the DAW and rescan plugins. In Logic, open Plug-in Manager and click Reset & Rescan. In Ableton, turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" in Preferences > Plug-Ins. Still missing? Email chef@pluginsforbreakfast.com with your DAW and system, and we'll fix it with you.

Your pantry: licences and downloads

Where are my licences and downloads?

Click Account at the top of our site and sign in with the email you bought with. Every plugin you own or are trying is there.

How many computers can I use?

Each licence works on 3 of your computers. Activation also works offline, for studio machines without internet.

I got a new computer (new kitchen!). What now?

Sign in to your account, remove the old computer from the licence, then activate on the new one. Stuck? Email us and we'll reset it.

Which DAWs and formats work?

On Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3 and AAX for Pro Tools. On Windows 10 and 11: VST3, 64-bit (no AAX on Windows). They work in Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage, Ableton Live, Pro Tools (Mac), FL Studio, Cubase, Nuendo, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig Studio, Reason, Digital Performer, Cakewalk Sonar (Windows), Waveform, Ardour, Mixbus, LUNA, Renoise and any other host that loads these formats. Butter Spread, our free plugin, is AU and VST3 for now. The formats for each plugin are listed in your account downloads.
