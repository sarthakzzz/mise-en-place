What is Popping Candy?

Popping Candy is a tempo-synced glitch plugin in a sweet-shop jar. Nine effects (repeat, reverse, tape stop, gate, pitch, filter, crush, scatter and reverb) pop on random steps in time with your song. Roll a new pattern with the gumball machine, lock the lids you love, and let the rest fizz.

What's on the plate

  • Jars The chance of each effect per step: repeat, reverse, tape stop, gate, pitch, filter, crush, scatter, reverb
  • Price tags Step length from 1/1 to 1/64, straight, triplet or dotted
  • Gumball machine Pop: roll a fresh pattern
  • Bowl Chaos: how busy it gets
  • Licorice lever Audio arpeggiator
  • Lids Lock a pattern you like

Great for

  • Fills and transitions in seconds
  • Vocal chops and stutters
  • Making a static loop sound like a remix

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
