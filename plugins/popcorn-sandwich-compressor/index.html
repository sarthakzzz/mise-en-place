What is Popcorn Sandwich Compressor?

Popcorn Sandwich is a one-knob compressor and expander. Turn the Pop dial left to squeeze (compression), right to pop things open (expansion); the centre does nothing at all. Pick from four flavours, Clean VCA, Punch FET, Smooth Opto and Warm Vari-Mu, and watch the live curve on the chalkboard as you go.

What's on the plate

  • Sandwich dial Pop: left compresses, right expands
  • Popcorn cartons Clean VCA, Punch FET, Smooth Opto or Warm Vari-Mu
  • Weights and sugar cube Ratio and knee
  • Sieve Ignore bass in the detector
  • Pot lid Soft ceiling
  • Popcorn bucket Mix, for instant parallel compression

Great for

  • Drums that need to come back to life (turn right)
  • Parallel compression with Mix at 50%
  • Vocals that need to sit still in the mix

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
