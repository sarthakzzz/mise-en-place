What is Spice Shelf EQ?

Spice Shelf EQ is an equalizer you play by taste. Five spice jars (Hearty, Creamy, Smoky, Zesty and Crispy) each season one part of your sound: lift a jar for more, lower it for less. A live EQ curve shows exactly what you did, and the mortar and pestle tames harsh peaks so bright mixes stay easy on the ears.

What's on the plate

  • Spice jars Hearty, Creamy, Smoky, Zesty, Crispy: add or cut each band
  • Pepper mill Frequency and width of the chosen spice
  • Screen Live EQ curve over your audio, with draggable points
  • Mortar and pestle Tame: softens harsh peaks
  • Presets Level matched, so you judge tone, not volume

Great for

  • Quick tone shaping on vocals, guitars and keys
  • Taming harsh, fizzy top end
  • Anyone who would rather taste than read frequency charts

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
