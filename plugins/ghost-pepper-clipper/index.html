What is Ghost Pepper Clipper?

Ghost Pepper Clipper is a clipper plugin for hot, clean loudness. Choose your heat from a soft bell pepper through jalapeño and habanero to a brickwall ghost pepper. Add smoke for saturation, keep the bass round with the bass saver, and taste exactly what is being clipped before you commit.

What's on the plate

  • Ghost pepper in the mortar Drive
  • Pepper rack Shape: bell soft, jalapeño, habanero, ghost brickwall
  • Hot sauce bottle Ceiling
  • Brazier Smoke: saturation before the clipper
  • Ice glass Bass saver: lows skip the clipper
  • Spoon Taste: hear only what is clipped
  • Spanner Oversampling

Great for

  • Loud drums without pumping
  • Mix bus loudness
  • Taming peaks before a limiter

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
