What is Butter Spread?

Butter Spread is our free stereo widener plugin, and it is open source too. Spread the knife for width from mono to 200%, shake in cinnamon for a mono-safe doubler, and pull the toaster lever to keep bass in mono. Free for Mac and Windows: join our morning menu email and it is yours.

What's on the plate

  • Knife on the toast Width from 0% (mono) to 200%
  • Cinnamon shaker Doubler that stays mono-safe
  • Toaster lever and dial Bass mono below 40 to 400 Hz
  • Teacup Output

Great for

  • Wider synths, pads and backing vocals
  • Keeping club and vinyl low end in mono
  • Anyone who wants a free plugin that is actually good

Works in your DAW

AU and VST3 on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Free, forever. Open source (AGPLv3).
