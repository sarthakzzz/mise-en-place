What is Butter Spread?

Butter Spread is our free stereo widener plugin, and it is open source too. Spread the knife for width from mono to 200%, shake in cinnamon for a mono-safe doubler, and pull the toaster lever to keep bass in mono. We're sending a few free licences to our customers, for Mac and Windows: sign up and one is yours.

What's on the plate

  • Knife on the toast Width from 0% (mono) to 200%
  • Cinnamon shaker Doubler that stays mono-safe
  • Toaster lever and dial Bass mono below 40 to 400 Hz
  • Teacup Output

Great for

  • Wider synths, pads and backing vocals
  • Keeping club and vinyl low end in mono
  • Anyone who wants a free licence for a plugin that is actually good
