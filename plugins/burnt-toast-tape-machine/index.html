What is Burnt Toast Tape Machine?

Burnt Toast Tape Machine is a tape saturation plugin in a toaster. Turn up the heat to drive into the tape and watch the toast brown. Pick modern, studio, vintage or cassette tape at 7.5, 15 or 30 ips, then add hiss, wow, flutter and head bump for warm tape character or an instant lo-fi loop.

What's on the plate

  • Heat dial Drive into the tape
  • Bread shelf Tape type: modern, studio, vintage, cassette
  • Toast rack Tape speed: 7.5, 15 or 30 ips
  • Agar jelly and crumbs Wow and flutter
  • Kettle Hiss
  • Bagel and butter Head bump and bias
  • Honey Mellow high cut

Great for

  • Warmth on drums, bass and full mixes
  • Lo-fi loops (cassette plus wow and flutter)
  • Gluing a mix with gentle tape drive

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
