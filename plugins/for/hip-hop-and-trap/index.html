Plugins for hip hop and trap

Trap and hip hop need the same few things every time: an 808 you can hear on a phone, drums that hit, a vocal that sits on top, and a loud master.

Saturate the 808 so its harmonics carry on small speakers, clip the drums so they can go louder, keep the vocal chain short, and master to a loud target. Add a stutter or tape stop before the drop and you are done.

All of these load in FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro and Pro Tools.

What to reach for

  1. Orange Crusher (distortion). Makes an 808 growl and cut through on small speakers, with a sub boost when it needs more weight.
  2. Ghost Pepper Clipper (clipper). The hard, loud drum sound: clip the kick and snare peaks cleanly.
  3. Popcorn Sandwich Compressor (compressor). Levels a rap vocal from one dial, or pops flat drum loops open.
  4. Spice Shelf EQ (equalizer). Carve space for the vocal and tame harsh hi-hats.
  5. Popping Candy (glitch and stutter). Stutters, reverses and tape stops for fills and beat switches.
  6. Spaghetti Delay (delay). Ducked delay throws on ad-libs.
  7. Cherry on the Top Mastering (mastering). A loud, clean master with a Loud target for club and car systems.

Questions

How do I make my 808 louder?

Add saturation so it has harmonics above the sub, then clip the peaks. Orange Crusher into Ghost Pepper Clipper does both.

Do these plugins work in FL Studio?

Yes. They are VST3 on Windows and Mac, plus AU and AAX on Mac. In FL Studio go to Options, Manage plugins, Find plugins.
