Plugins for drums that hit

Loud, punchy drums come from three moves: shape the transient, add harmonics, and clip the peaks cleanly. These plugins do those jobs with one or two controls each.

On a drum bus, start with the compressor turned towards Pop to bring the hits forward, add saturation for weight, and finish with the clipper so the peaks stop eating your headroom. On single drums, a gate tightens kicks and toms in seconds.

For 808s and bass, saturation is what makes them heard on small speakers. Add it before the clipper, not after.

What to reach for

  1. Ghost Pepper Clipper (clipper). Shaves the peaks off kicks and snares so the whole beat can go louder without a limiter pumping.
  2. Popcorn Sandwich Compressor (compressor). Turn right and it expands: flat drums pop back to life. Turn left for glue. Mix at 50% gives instant parallel compression.
  3. Orange Crusher (distortion). Drive, transient punch and a sub boost in one window. Made for drum buses and 808s.
  4. Gummy Gate (gate). Tightens kicks and toms, or works as a trance gate in time with your track.
  5. Burnt Toast Tape Machine (tape saturation). Tape saturation that rounds off harsh hi-hats and glues a kit together.
  6. Popping Candy (glitch and stutter). Stutters, repeats and tape stops on random steps for fills and transitions.
  7. Bread Oven Reverb (reverb). A real room around a dry kit, with a short decay so it stays punchy.

Questions

How do I make drums hit harder?

Bring the transients forward with expansion or parallel compression, add saturation for weight, then clip the peaks. Popcorn Sandwich Compressor, Orange Crusher and Ghost Pepper Clipper are that chain.

What does a clipper do on drums?

It cuts the very top of each hit cleanly. The drums sound the same, but the peaks are lower, so the whole track can be louder.
