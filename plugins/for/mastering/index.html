Mastering plugins that keep it simple

Mastering at home is about three things: a balanced tone, the right loudness for streaming, and no nasty peaks. You do not need twenty plugins for that.

The short version: fix tone with a gentle EQ, add a little tape if the mix feels stiff, clip the loudest peaks, and let the mastering plugin bring it to your loudness target. Compare at matched volume, because louder always sounds better at first.

Cherry on the Top learns your level, lets you pick a target (Apple, Spotify, Loud, Club or your own) and does the rest from one dial.

What to reach for

  1. Cherry on the Top Mastering (mastering). The whole finish from one dial: multiband control, glue, true-peak ceiling and loudness targets.
  2. Ghost Pepper Clipper (clipper). Clip a decibel or two off the peaks before the master so the limiter works less.
  3. Spice Shelf EQ (equalizer). Small tone moves on the whole mix, with level-matched presets so you judge the tone and not the volume.
  4. Burnt Toast Tape Machine (tape saturation). Gentle tape glue when a mix sounds like separate tracks.
  5. Butter Spread (stereo widener). Check the width, and keep the low end in mono for clubs and vinyl.

Questions

Can I master a song with one plugin?

For demos and most releases, yes. Cherry on the Top Mastering handles tone, glue, loudness and a true-peak ceiling from one dial.

How loud should my master be for Spotify and Apple Music?

Around -14 LUFS for Spotify and -16 LUFS for Apple Music is where they play things back. Cherry on the Top has both as targets, plus louder ones for club tracks.

Can I try them before buying?

Yes. Every plugin has a 3-day free trial, and Butter Spread, our stereo widener, comes with a free licence.

Which formats do they come in?

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac (Apple-notarised installers) and VST3 on Windows 10 and 11.
