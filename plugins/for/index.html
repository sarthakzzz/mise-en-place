What are you cooking?

Pick the job, the style or the DAW, and we will hand you the right plugins in the right order.

  • Lo-fi plugins, toasted to taste
    Lo-fi is mostly four things: tape warmth, a little pitch wobble, less top end, and echoes that sound older than the song. These are the plugins we reach for when a track is too clean for its own good.
  • A vocal chain you can cook in five minutes
    A good vocal chain is short: clean it, shape it, level it, then give it some space. Here is ours, in the order we use it.
  • Plugins for drums that hit
    Loud, punchy drums come from three moves: shape the transient, add harmonics, and clip the peaks cleanly. These plugins do those jobs with one or two controls each.
  • Mastering plugins that keep it simple
    Mastering at home is about three things: a balanced tone, the right loudness for streaming, and no nasty peaks. You do not need twenty plugins for that.
  • Mixing plugins for beginners
    Most plugins look like aeroplane cockpits. Ours look like kitchens, and each one has a single main control that does the right thing. If you are new to mixing, start with these five.
  • Creative effects you play like toys
    Some plugins fix problems. These make new ones, on purpose: stutters, tape stops, grains, sweeps and movement, all in time with your track.
  • Plugins for hip hop and trap
    Trap and hip hop need the same few things every time: an 808 you can hear on a phone, drums that hit, a vocal that sits on top, and a loud master.
  • Plugins for FL Studio
    All 16 of our plugins run in FL Studio as VST3, on Windows 10 and 11 and on Mac. Here is how to get them showing up, and which ones to try first.
  • Plugins for Logic Pro
    All 16 of our plugins are Audio Units, so they load straight into Logic Pro. Every Mac installer is notarised by Apple, so it opens without a security warning.
  • Plugins for Ableton Live
    We test every plugin in Ableton Live. All 16 load as VST3 on Mac and Windows, and as Audio Units on Mac.
  • Plugins for Pro Tools
    Our paid plugins come as AAX for Pro Tools on Mac, signed so Pro Tools loads them without complaint. The same installer adds AU and VST3 for your other DAWs.
