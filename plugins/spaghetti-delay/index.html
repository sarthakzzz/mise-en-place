What is Spaghetti Delay?

Spaghetti Delay is a delay plugin rolled out of a pasta machine. Choose clean, tape, analog or lo-fi echoes, sync them to your song, and watch each repeat appear as a cherry tomato. Add wobble, drive, ping-pong and ducking, or freeze the last repeats into a loop.

What's on the plate

  • Pasta machine crank Delay time
  • Pantry shelf Character: clean, tape, analog or lo-fi
  • Metronome Sync to tempo, from 1/1 to 1/32, dotted or triplet
  • Fork twirl Feedback
  • Tagliatelle and chillies Wobble and drive
  • Plates Ping-pong
  • Cloche and gelato Ducking and freeze

Great for

  • Vocal throws that stay out of the way (ducking)
  • Dub-style tape echoes
  • Turning a phrase into a loop with Freeze

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
