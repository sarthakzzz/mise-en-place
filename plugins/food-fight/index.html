What is Food Fight?

Food Fight is a performance multi-effect you play like an instrument. Hold a food to throw its effect: Splat, Pump, Spin, Freeze, Smear, Talk, Clang or Whoosh, all in time with your song. Let the catapult throw foods by itself for happy chaos, or automate the holds to arrange your own mess.

What's on the plate

  • Foods Hold to throw: Splat, Pump, Spin, Freeze, Smear, Talk, Clang, Whoosh
  • Food tags How hard each food hits
  • Catapult Chaos: throws foods by itself
  • Egg timer Rate and swing
  • Tempo Follows your DAW, or set your own BPM

Great for

  • Live sets and jamming
  • Build-ups, drops and transitions
  • Arranged chaos, by automating the holds

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
