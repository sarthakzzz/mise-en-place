What is Muesli Granular?

Muesli Granular is a granular effect plugin that cuts your audio into tiny grains and scatters them over a breakfast bowl. Grains can be plain, reversed, pitched up or down, or stuttered. Freeze a moment into a pad, drizzle in feedback and add sparkle for shimmer.

What's on the plate

  • Oat canister Grain size
  • Bowl Density, free or synced
  • Cloud Scatter
  • Spoon Stir: where in the past it reads
  • Milk jug Freeze
  • Honey dipper Drizzle: feedback
  • Dishes Plain, reverse, -12, +7 and stutter grains

Great for

  • Instant pads (freeze plus a little scatter)
  • Shimmer (sparkle plus drizzle)
  • Turning one-shots into evolving textures

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
