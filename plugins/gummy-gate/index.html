What is Gummy Gate?

Gummy Gate is a gate, expander, ducker and rhythmic chopper in one sweet jar. Drag the licorice lace to set where the gate opens, filter the key with the sieve so only the right sounds get in, and switch to chop mode to turn any pad into a rhythm in time with your song.

What's on the plate

  • Licorice lace Threshold
  • Shelf Mode: gate, soft expander, ducker or chop
  • Sieve Key filter
  • Trampoline, bear and worm Attack, hold and release
  • Spyglass Lookahead
  • Tin-can phone Listen to the key

Great for

  • Tight drums and clean tom tracks
  • Trance-gate pads
  • Ducking music under a voice

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
