What is Milkshake Chorus?

Milkshake Chorus is a thick, creamy chorus plugin. Four flavours (clean, vintage, tape and ensemble), one to four straws of voices, tempo sync, and whipped-cream high cut to keep it smooth. Two shakes of width and it is ready to serve.

What's on the plate

  • Mixer speed dial and swirl Rate and depth
  • Straws Voices, 1 to 4
  • Flavour shelf Clean, vintage, tape or ensemble
  • Jukebox Sync to tempo
  • Two shakes Width
  • Whipped cream and wafers High cut and low cut
  • Cherry Vibrato

Great for

  • Classic synth chorus (vintage, two straws)
  • Wide guitars and keys
  • Thickening vocals and pads

Works in your DAW

AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac, VST3 on Windows. Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, GarageBand and more. macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).
