How it works

You pressed Activate in the plugin, which brought you here. Sign in, then choose which of your plugins to unlock on this computer: all of them, a few, or only that one. Once it says unlocked, go back to your DAW: they are ready, even ones that are already open. No internet on that computer? In the plugin choose offline activation, then bring the file here and press Activate.
