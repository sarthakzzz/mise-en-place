Plugins for Breakfast is a British audio software company founded by Escaé Studios in November 2026 and based in London. The company designs and sells audio plugins (software effects used inside digital audio workstations such as Logic Pro, Ableton Live, FL Studio and Pro Tools) for music producers. Its plugins are known for their user interfaces: each one is drawn as a hand-made, needle-felt miniature food scene, where every control is a small object that is played like a toy.

The company launched with a range of 16 plugins covering distortion, EQ, compression, delay, reverb, modulation, filtering, tape saturation, granular processing and mastering. One of them, the stereo widener Butter Spread, is free and open source.

History

Plugins for Breakfast was set up in November 2026 by Escaé Studios as its audio software brand. Its tagline is "We make delicious plugins", and it calls itself "the most important plug-in of the day".

The company opened its online store at pluginsforbreakfast.com in November 2026 with all 16 plugins available at once, rather than releasing them one by one. It announced plans to add a new plugin to the range every month.

Products

All plugins run as Audio Units (AU), VST3 and AAX on macOS and as VST3 on Windows. Each has level-matched factory presets, full undo and redo, smoothed (click-free) controls and, where timing matters, tempo sync from whole notes to 1/32 notes, including dotted and triplet values.

Design

Every Plugins for Breakfast interface is a three-dimensional scene that looks as if it were made of felt and wool. Instead of rows of knobs, the controls are objects in the scene: a knife spreads butter to widen the stereo image, spice jars are lifted to boost frequencies, and noodles are raised to open a filter. Most plugins keep advanced settings behind a single spanner icon so the main view stays simple. The aim, in the company's words, is "plugins you smile at before you even hear them".

Business model

The plugins are sold directly from the company's website, each with a free three-day trial. At launch, single plugins started at US$19, the full set of 16 (the "Full Fridge" bundle) at US$50, and a yearly subscription (the "Breakfast Club") at US$75. Launch prices rise in small steps after every 100 copies sold, so early buyers pay the least.

Open source

Butter Spread is free for everyone and its source code is published under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 (AGPLv3).
