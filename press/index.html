Breakfast is served: Plugins for Breakfast opens its kitchen with 16 audio plugins made of felt

Plugins for Breakfast, a new audio software brand founded by Escaé Studios, has opened its doors with a full menu of 16 audio plugins for music producers. Each plugin is a hand-made needle-felt miniature world where every control is a little object you play like a toy: an orange that crushes your drums, a delay made of spaghetti, a reverb that lives inside a bread oven.

Under the wool sit serious tools. Every plugin comes with level-matched factory presets, full undo and redo, smooth click-free controls and tempo sync wherever timing matters, in AU, VST3 and AAX on Mac and VST3 on Windows.

"We wanted plugins you smile at before you even hear them, and then they sound great. Think of it as the most important plug-in of the day."
The Chef, Plugins for Breakfast

Price and availability

Available now at pluginsforbreakfast.com, with a free 3-day trial of every plugin. Plugins start at $19 each, all 16 together (the Full Fridge) start at $50, and the Breakfast Club starts at $75 a year. Early prices step up a little after every 100 sold, so the first cooks eat cheapest. Butter Spread, a stereo widener, is free and open source. A new plugin joins the menu every month.
